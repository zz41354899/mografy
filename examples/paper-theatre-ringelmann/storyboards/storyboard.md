# Storyboard — 林格曼效應 Ringelmann Effect (v1, timed to the narration)

- **Length:** about 79 s (final timing follows the recorded narration) · **Aspect:** 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps · puppets and props on 2s, needles eased, 2D marks redrawn every 2 frames · **Copy:** Traditional Chinese
- **Film idea:** 桌上紙劇場裡只有一條麻繩，一端綁在黃銅測力錶上。紙偶一個個加入拉繩，錶上的紅色指針是 3D 的「實際」，2D 金色虛線指針是「應該要到」，兩根針之間的落差，就是這支片要講的效應。第二幕同一個錶變成音量計；第三幕繩子拆成四股，每個人拿到自己的那一股、自己的那個錶。
- **World:** the same desk paper theatre as 陰影心理學 / 格式塔心理學 / 美即好用效應 (box x ±6, y 0..7.6, z 0..7; brass stand lamp; fibre paper; DOF, grain .045, vignette .4).
- **Layers:** 3D theatre (twine rope, eight paper puppets, brass dynamometer, real light) · 2D "should be" layer (gold dashed needle, force arrows above heads) · 2D hand-drawn data (chalk bars, dashed rings, question marks, sound arcs).
- **Rule:** one rope and one dial appear in every shot. The camera passes through the dial glass into full-frame 2D (S06) and the rope itself becomes 2D in S10; four strands come back as four short ropes in S11.

## Sources checked

- Ringelmann (1913), data gathered in the 1880s; per-person output 93 % (2), 85 % (3), 49 % (8) from his table, as re-analysed by Kravitz & Martin (1986), who note that table's task is not specified as rope pulling.
- Ingham, Levinger, Graves & Peckham (1974): blindfolded participants with confederates who only pretended to pull still pulled less.
- Latané, Williams & Harkins (1979): shouting with one believed partner 82 %, with five 74 %; named social loafing.
- Karau & Williams (1993): meta-analysis of 78 studies; loafing falls when contributions are identifiable, the task is meaningful, groups are small and members value the group.
