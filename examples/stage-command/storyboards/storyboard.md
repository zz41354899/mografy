# Storyboard — stage-command

6.5 s loop, 16:9, light, keyboard only.

| Time | Beat | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1.3 s | ⌘K | Keycaps press; the palette opens over a dimmed page. |
| 1.3–2.0 s | Type | "assign" filters the list to two commands. |
| 2.0–4.6 s | Assign | ↵: the avatar flies into Assignee; the camera leans in and out. |
| 4.6–6.5 s | ⌘Z | Undo returns the issue to Unassigned. |

Times come from the script in `scenes/main.ts` (`play()` decides them from cursor distance and holds); `meta.shots` comes from its `mark()` steps.
