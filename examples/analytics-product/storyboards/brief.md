# Creative brief

Create an 8-second motion design demo that explains a fictional analytics product through one clear interaction.

Use a warm-white background, dark typography, subtle gray surfaces, and teal #12B5A5 accents. Keep the composition spacious, polished, and readable at thumbnail size.

Begin with a minimal dashboard containing a sidebar, one metric, and a small bar chart. A cursor selects “This week.” The metric counts up and the chart smoothly updates. Gently focus the composition on the changed result while secondary UI becomes quieter.

Use restrained movement, coordinated timing, and smooth easing. Hold the completed state for the final 2 seconds. Design a clean transition back to the beginning.

Avoid dense dashboard details, excessive text, decorative particles, and dramatic camera movement. Do not include video controls, website card labels, or audio.

## Production settings

16:9 landscape, 1920 × 1080, 8 seconds, 30 fps, silent MP4. All names and numbers are fictional. The final frame matches the first, so the film loops without a cut.
