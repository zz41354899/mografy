# Storyboard — stage-calendar

7.5 s loop, 16:9, light, browser chrome.

| Time | Beat | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1.4 s | Lean in | The camera pushes in on Tue–Wed; the hand moves to the card. |
| 1.4–2.6 s | Carry | The card lifts and follows; the target jumps cell to cell; drop on Wed 2 PM. |
| 2.6–4.6 s | Confirm | Three attendees confirm one after another; the camera returns. |
| 4.6–7.5 s | Back | The card is dragged back to Tuesday; the hand goes home. |

Times come from the script in `scenes/main.ts` (`play()` decides them from cursor distance and holds); `meta.shots` comes from its `mark()` steps.
