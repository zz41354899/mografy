# Creative brief

Create an 8-second brand introduction for Mografy using the supplied official logo.

Use a deep-charcoal background, the original logo colors, and restrained teal #12B5A5 accents. Preserve the logo’s exact shape and proportions.

Begin with a quiet frame. Introduce a short teal motion gesture that leads naturally into the logo reveal. Reveal the mark first, followed by the Mografy wordmark with a slight timing offset. Let both settle into a strong, centered composition.

Make the motion confident, precise, and memorable, with gentle anticipation and a controlled settle. Keep the complete logo clearly visible for the final 2 seconds, then transition cleanly into the next loop.

Avoid redesigning the logo, excessive glow, generic particle explosions, and unrelated decorative objects. Do not include video controls, website card labels, or audio.

## Production settings

16:9 landscape, 1920 × 1080, 8 seconds, 30 fps, silent MP4. The film uses the official logo paths with the inverse white ink on charcoal; the ribbon keeps #12B5A5. The final frame matches the first, so the film loops without a cut.
