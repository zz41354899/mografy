# Storyboard — mografy-brand

| Time | Beat | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1.8s | Lead with a gesture | After a quiet beat, one teal stroke enters and traces the ribbon of the mark, revealing it as it travels. |
| 1.2–3.6s | Build the lockup | The arches rise with a slight dip and a controlled settle; the mark moves left and the wordmark follows letter by letter. |
| 3.6–8s | Let it read | The complete logo holds for more than three seconds, then fades to the same quiet frame the loop begins with. |

Review poses are listed in `mografy.json` under `review`. Run `mografy review` and inspect the stills and transition strips before export.
