# 來自月亮的偶像 — Lunar Pop Club story (storyboard v1, built)

A 60 s story told inside the same paper toy theatre as the 25 s promo (`paper-theatre-moon-rabbit`): a rabbit who lives on the moon sees the lonely lights of the town below, comes down along a moonbeam, sings to an empty hall, and the light she brings fills it one penlight at a time, until she becomes the moon idol.

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 60 s. Props, flats and the puppet on 2s (12 poses/s) with a seeded boil; camera, light and type smooth.
- **Telling:** on-screen story cards only (no narration): two-column vertical cards lowered on strings, read right column first. Each card holds at least 3 s.
- **Idea in one line:** moonlight travels — from the moon to the town's windows, down the moonbeam she rides, into one spotlight on an empty stage, out into the audience's penlights, and back as the full stage.
- **Music:** an all-new original score (nothing from the promo track): `assets/music.mp3`, 60 s, 120 BPM, E♭ major, bar 2 s, generated by `music-src/compose.py` (deterministic; `--stems` writes per-instrument WAVs). Music-box theme alone (序), pizzicato and window twinkles (起), strings, falling harp and flute for the descent with a soft landing on 23.5 (承), one held note and sparse C-minor piano in the empty hall (轉), her verse on a soft synth voice with one blip per arriving fan, claps, kick and a riser (32–40), the idol chorus with a new hook from 40 (drums, octave bass, pumping chords, bells; whooshes on the card pulls), and the music-box theme returning for the curtain call with the final chord on 58. Chords avoid the IV–V–iii–vi "royal road" progression. Timeline: `storyboards/music-timeline.png`.
- **Assets:** the paper theatre and every paper piece from the promo; `character.webp` as the puppet (its moon side is her own picture printed in lavender duotone = the moon rabbit; the full-colour face = the idol), its plum shadow for the descent; `end-stage.png` backdrop; portraits for the chapter cards. New pieces: the moon rabbit's mortar and mallet, a paper town with cut-out windows, a paper parasol, cloud shadows, a row of empty seats, audience silhouettes with penlights, paper music notes, the two-column story cards.

## Story

| Act | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 起 | Long ago a rabbit lived on the moon. Every night she watched the lights of the town below: under each one, someone who couldn't sleep. |
| 承 | So she decided to go and keep them company. She floats down a moonbeam under a paper parasol. |
| 轉 | Her first stage, and not one person in the seats. She sings anyway. One penlight, then two… |
| 合 | The hall fills, the stage lights up and she turns to her full colours: the moon idol. Her nights (登場, 魅力, 節拍), and a curtain call: 今晚，陪伴著你。 |

## Files

- `storyboards/storyboard.html` + `board.ts` / `board.js` — the visual storyboard, drawn with the real theatre and the new pieces in `scenes/story.ts`.
- `scenes/main.ts` is the story film; `scenes/promo.ts` is the promo scene kept as a helper module (chapter cards, marquee, drapes); `scenes/story.ts` holds the story's pieces.
- `storyboards/storyboard-story-v1-page-1.png`, `-page-2.png` — rendered pages.

## Assumptions

- Kept 120 BPM and the promo's design system so the story cuts on the same bar grid and the last 20 s reuse the promo's set pieces. The music is new; `mografy.json` switches to `assets/music.mp3` when the story scene replaces the promo scene.
- The puppet's lavender duotone side reads as "the moon rabbit" and its full-colour face as "the idol"; the flip on 40 is the story's turn. (In this project the back face is the duotone; the 25 s promo keeps its plain lavender back.)
- Copy is a first draft in the brief's tone; every line is a separate card so it's easy to rewrite.
