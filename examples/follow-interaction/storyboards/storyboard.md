# Storyboard — follow-interaction

| Time | Beat | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1.8s | Anticipate the click | The cursor approaches on a gentle arc and pauses while the button lifts into its hover state. |
| 1.8–3.0s | Confirm the response | The press compresses the button, a soft ripple spreads, the label turns to Following with a check, and the confirmation springs up. |
| 3.0–8s | Hold and reset | The cursor steps aside, the result stays readable, then everything returns to Follow for the next loop. |

Review poses are listed in `mografy.json` under `review`. Run `mografy review` and inspect the stills and transition strips before export.
