# Creative brief

Create an 8-second motion design demo showing a satisfying interface interaction.

Use a pale-mint background, white UI surfaces, dark typography, and teal #12B5A5 accents. Center one clearly readable “Follow” button with a small profile avatar.

A cursor approaches the button, pauses briefly, and clicks. Show a subtle press response, then smoothly transform the button into a “Following” state with a checkmark. A small confirmation message appears beneath it: “You’re following.”

Coordinate the cursor, button response, and confirmation so the cause and effect are immediately clear. Use restrained spring motion and a few small secondary accents. Hold the result, then reset smoothly for looping.

Avoid full application screens, multiple competing interactions, large confetti bursts, and excessive text. Do not include video controls, website card labels, or audio.

## Production settings

16:9 landscape, 1920 × 1080, 8 seconds, 30 fps, silent MP4. All names and numbers are fictional. The final frame matches the first, so the film loops without a cut.
